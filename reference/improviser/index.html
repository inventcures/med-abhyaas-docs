    Improviser¶

    app.encounter.improviser ¶

    Bounded, plausible answers to reasonable questions the case does not script.

    A real patient answers "does it come with chills?" even when the case never mentions chills. The improviser may only give (1) a negative the case explicitly permits, (2) an everyday, non-clinical detail consistent with the persona, or (3) "I don't know". It never sees the diagnosis, differential, kernel, rubric or critical rules, and a validator rejects any number, medicine, test, diagnosis or positive symptom. Accepted answers are pinned so the patient never contradicts itself; pinned claims are never clinical evidence.

    permitted_negatives(case, rules=()) ¶

    Negatives this case allows: authored per case, plus reviewer-allowed topics; minus forbidden ones.

    allowed_classes(case, enforced) ¶

    Under the enforced faculty gate, everyday improvisation is off until reviewed.

    validate(result, case, state, negatives, classes) ¶

    Return a safe improvisation or None. Pure: used before speaking and again after the fact.

    improvise(improviser, question, topic, case, state, rules=(), enforced=False, actor=None, eid=None, gate=None) ¶

    A validated improvisation. Permitted negatives and pinned answers never reach generation; a generated sentence must pass the lexicon validator and, when configured, the pre-speech gate.

    pin(state, result, sequence) ¶

    Remember accepted claims so later answers stay consistent.

    for_encounter(improviser, store, db_or_none, actor, state, question, topic, eid=None) ¶

    Improvise using this case's reviewer rules and the faculty gate.

    app.improvisations ¶

    Faculty review of improvised answers: promote, allow as a negative, or forbid.

    Reviewers see every improvised claim per case. Promoting a claim creates a new draft case version with it as an authored fact; allowing a negative adds the topic to that case's permitted negatives at runtime; forbidding makes the patient answer "I don't know" instead.

    OPERATIONS = frozenset({'improvisations', 'improvisation_decision'}) module-attribute ¶

    DECISIONS = ('promote', 'allow_negative', 'forbid') module-attribute ¶
