    Pre-speech gating¶

    app.gating ¶

    Pre-speech gating of improvised answers with low-latency decision models.

    The lexicon validator in app/encounter/improviser.py always runs first. A decision model (Cloudflare Clef by default; Clef-flash, TypeSafe Jev or a self-hosted MuRIL classifier are interchangeable behind the same System One schema) then judges the candidate sentence; either signal blocks. GATE_MODE=off|shadow|enforce: shadow records what would have been blocked, enforce replaces blocked or unchecked answers with "I don't know". See docs/research/pre-speech-gating.md for why Clef is the default and why Hindi quality must be measured before enforcing.

    THRESHOLD = 0.5 module-attribute ¶

    app.gating_eval ¶

    Offline evaluation of pre-speech gates on a labelled improvisation set.

    The bundled set is synthetic: every item is labelled by how it was constructed (allow: a negated permitted topic, "don't know" or an everyday detail; block: a symptom the patient has, a number, a medicine, a diagnosis or a test). It seeds the comparison until clinicians label a real set (target ≥1,500 items, two annotators, Cohen's kappa).

    python3 -m app.gating_eval --model lexicon
python3 -m app.gating_eval --model clef        # needs CLOUDFLARE_ACCOUNT_ID and CLOUDFLARE_AI_TOKEN

    app.verbatim_tts ¶

    Speak an already-checked sentence exactly, for improvised turns on the Gemini Live path.

    Gemini Live cannot be made to read text verbatim; Gemini 3.8 TTS treats its input strictly as a transcript. It is asked for the case's Live voice name; Google does not state that TTS and Live voices are identical, so voice continuity needs a listening check. Sarvam bulbul:v3 in the persona's speaker is the fallback. Enabled with LIVE_VERBATIM_TTS=on; any failure falls back to letting Live speak the approved sentence (checked again afterwards).

    app.gemini_speech ¶

    Gemini text, transcription and speech for the request/response voice path.

    Gemini 3.8 TTS reads its input verbatim and accepts inline pause tags, so the #26 speech plan is rendered as tags instead of joined clips. It returns headerless 24 kHz 16-bit PCM, which the browser cannot decode, so audio is wrapped as WAV. Every failure raises ValueError so callers can fall back to Sarvam.

    pause_tags(text, plan) ¶

    Text with the speech plan's pauses as Gemini TTS tags, and the number of tags used.

    An ellipsis becomes a medium pause (long when the turn is hesitant); in a disfluent turn a clause comma becomes a short pause. A tag is dropped when the next word is a negation or a number, so a pause never separates or announces clinical content.
