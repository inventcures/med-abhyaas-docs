    Practice progress¶

    app.progress ¶

    Longitudinal practice progress per specialty track, and the evidence-banded session debrief.

    The practice score is a transparent, formative summary of the nine domain states (ADR 0001): evidenced 1, partly 0.5, not yet 0, safety concern 0 and capped below the pass line; suspended domains are left out. Attempts with hints are drawn but never enter the rolling mean, because supported performance is not independent performance. Learners are compared only with their own earlier attempts.

    OPERATIONS = frozenset({'progress_series'}) module-attribute ¶

    CREDIT = {'evidenced': 1.0, 'partially_evidenced': 0.5, 'not_yet_evidenced': 0.0, 'safety_concern': 0.0} module-attribute ¶

    MIN_JUDGED = 5 module-attribute ¶

    ROLLING = 5 module-attribute ¶

    RETEST_GAP = timedelta(days=7) module-attribute ¶

    KINDS = ('unassisted', 'with_hints', 'retest') module-attribute ¶

    BANDS = (('strong', 'Already strong', ('evidenced',)), ('developing', 'Getting there', ('partially_evidenced',)), ('work_on_next', 'Work on next', ('safety_concern', 'not_yet_evidenced')), ('not_judged', 'Not judged', ('suspended',))) module-attribute ¶

    FORMULA = 'Evidenced 1, partly 0.5, not yet 0 over the judged domains, x100. Suspended domains are left out; a safety concern caps the score below the pass line.' module-attribute ¶
