    Speech plans¶

    app.encounter.speech_plan ¶

    Per-turn speech plans: how long the patient waits, how disfluent, what shape and how certain.

    Real patients answer after short gaps, slow down for recall, wait longer before denials, sensitive disclosures and "I don't know", hedge more often than they sound sure, and are disfluent in bursts (many turns stay clean). See docs/research/patient-speech.md. Plans are deterministic per encounter and turn, recorded on the event, and never change facts: the guard checks the realized wording keeps every number, duration and negation.

    LATENCY_MS = {'simple': (150, 400), 'ordinary': (300, 800), 'recall': (800, 1500), 'dispreferred': (700, 2000)} module-attribute ¶

    BUDGET_WEIGHTS = ((0, 0.45), (1, 0.3), (2, 0.17), (3, 0.08)) module-attribute ¶

    SHAPES = ('minimal', 'minimal_expansion', 'narrative', 'off_target', 'trailing', 'dont_know') module-attribute ¶

    SENSITIVE_FACTS = {'tobacco', 'smoking', 'alcohol', 'background', 'fears', 'concern', 'belief', 'wishes', 'meds', 'medication', 'medications', 'other_drugs'} module-attribute ¶

    plan(case, state, encounter, sequence, content, fact_id=None, answer_class=None, affect=None, condition=None) ¶

    The speech plan for one patient turn. Neutral audio and text conditions keep timing but drop disfluency.

    instruction(p) ¶

    Realizer guidance for this turn; it shapes wording only, never facts.

    guard(text, permitted) ¶

    Reasons the realized wording is unsafe; empty when every number, duration and negation survives.

    segments(text, max_segments=3) ¶

    Clause segments for segmented synthesis, split at pauses and clause punctuation.

    text_statistics(replies) ¶

    Disfluency and length statistics from reply text alone, for baselines without plans.

    Session speech statistics against the research targets.
