    Case authoring schema¶

    Cases live in app/cases_draft/*.json (general practice) and app/cases_oncology/*.json. This page documents fields, not case content.

    Top-level fields¶

    Field Audience Meaning
    id, title, schema_version, status All Identity and draft status (demonstration_* for unreviewed drafts).
    specialty Progress Specialty track: family_medicine (non-specialist consultations such as fevers and general complaints), infectious_disease, pulmonology, gastroenterology, endocrinology, oncology or palliative_care (app/specialties.py). The compiler rejects any other value. Pending clinician confirmation.
    persona Authoring, speech style Independent dimensions: age, gender, occupation, area, language, register, literacy, health literacy, household, constraints. Never infer one from another.
    opening Patient What the patient says first.
    patient_knowledge Patient policy What the patient knows and does not know.
    speech_style Realizer Conversational variation, independent of demographics.
    voice Speech sarvam_speaker and gemini_voice (validated against provider lists, matched to the speaker's gender), baseline_affect, affect_range, pace range within 0.5–2.0.
    facts[] Patient policy See below.
    permitted_negatives Improviser Topics the patient may deny when asked (id from the shared library, about, triggers, review). Never a feature the case's truth makes plausible.
    attendant Speech, policy Optional family member or caregiver: relation, gender, a voice that must differ from the patient's, facts[] (same shape, plus optional interject), review.
    examination Examiner channel granularity (ask_to_specify or general_survey) and components keyed by system (vitals, chest, ...).
    exam, tests Examiner channel Raw authored vitals and test results; every test key must map to a concept in services.KEY_CONCEPTS.
    kernel Assessor only clue, followup, unlocks, miss, plus fact (the patient fact carrying the clue) and use_actions (authored exams/orders that count as using it).
    critical_rules Assessor only Deterministic required / unsafe rules: match on facts, action types, released items_any, text_any, with optional unless_facts.
    truth, timeline, ddx, kernel, actions, unsafe, closure, assessment, review Assessor only Never sent to a patient realizer.

    Paper personas¶

    Three draft cases implement the personas in the paper's figure (app/cases_draft/persona_*.json, cohort Clinical practice):

    Persona Case Notes
    A persona_a_kiln_cough 50-year-old woman, brick-kiln worker, western Uttar Pradesh; Western Hindi register; persistent cough whose household-contact clue must be elicited
    B persona_b_kundli_diabetes 65-year-old farmer from Kundli, Haryana; Haryanvi/Hindi code-mixing; glycaemic decompensation with a painless infected foot ulcer; his son interjects about missed tablets
    C persona_c_bengali_goals_of_care 62-year-old Bengali-speaking retired teacher; difficult goals-of-care conversation; his daughter interrupts with a different concern. The catalogue suggests Bengali (suggested_language) and the Hindi source is realized in Bengali

    Register is chosen by faculty independently of literacy, income or occupation; dialect negations such as कोनी are protected like नहीं. All three are pending clinician and native-speaker review.

    Facts¶

    {
  "id": "intake_output",
  "disclosure": "Targeted hydration",
  "patient_known": true,
  "requires": [],
  "affect": "worried",
  "about": "fever: when it started, how high, what helps",
  "attributes": {
    "max_temperature": {"about": "how high the fever goes", "triggers": ["बुखार कितना", "fever kitna", "thermometer"],
                        "patient_voice": {"hi-IN": {"levels": ["..."], "review": "draft_..."}}}
  },
  "content": "Clinical note for faculty and assessment",
  "patient_voice": {
    "hi-IN": {
      "levels": ["vague gist", "characterized detail", "precise detail"],
      "review": "draft_pending_native_speaker_and_clinician_review"
    }
  }
}

    Rules enforced by the compiler:

    • Fact ids are unique; requires refers only to existing facts and has no cycles.
    • Every fact has 1–3 non-empty patient_voice["hi-IN"].levels, gist first. Each level adds new detail rather than repeating earlier ones; together they cover the clinical note.
    • Patient voice is first person, uses lay quantities (fever in °F, approximate durations) and contains no clinical shorthand.

    A rendering becomes reviewed when its review is approved. Case-level review.clinical, review.language and review.source_applicability work the same way.
